## Data residency (Southeast Asia)

A new requirement takes effect within the next two quarters: customer data processed by AI-driven systems operating in this region must be stored in-region, not transferred out for processing. This is directly relevant to any proposal involving customer-facing AI tooling in Southeast Asia — proposals should state their data storage location explicitly, not leave it implied.

## AI governance disclosure

A regional guideline now expects documented human-review checkpoints for AI-driven decisions above a defined size-of-price threshold, with an auditable record of who reviewed and when. Proposals built with a human-in-the-loop approval step and a written decision trail are already positioned to meet this — proposals that fully automate decisions above the threshold without a review checkpoint should flag this explicitly for PMO.
