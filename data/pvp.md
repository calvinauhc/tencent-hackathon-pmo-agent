## Core Values

1. **Integrity** — proposals must represent risk and financial projections honestly; no material omission of known risk to secure approval.
2. **Customer-first** — initiatives should trace to a defined customer or internal-user benefit, not process for its own sake.
3. **Sustainability** — long-term margin health is weighed above short-term revenue capture.
4. **Accountable innovation** — new technology adoption (including AI) requires a named owner accountable for outcomes, not just an idea sponsor.

## Ethics Guidelines

- No project may proceed if it requires circumventing known regulatory requirements in its operating region.
- Data privacy: any project handling customer or employee data must specify its data-handling approach at intake; proposals silent on data handling are treated as incomplete, not compliant-by-default.
- Fair dealing: proposals must not misrepresent competitive position, market size, or existing internal capability to secure faster approval.

## Working Principles

- **Evidence-based decisions**: financial and risk claims should be traceable to a stated basis (market data, internal benchmark, named assumption) — unsupported claims are flagged, not rejected outright, and routed for clarification.
- **Transparency**: PMO rejection or review decisions must be explained in terms the requester can act on, not just "declined."
- **Regulatory caution**: unknown or unquantified regulatory risk defaults to "under review," never auto-approved, regardless of financial upside.
